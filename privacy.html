Privacy notice

Read this first. This describes what the software actually does with your information — each statement corresponds to a rule the database enforces, not to an intention. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should be before these platforms are opened to the public.

Lesotho's Data Protection Act 2011 applies to us. So, for anyone in South Africa, does POPIA. This notice is written to be plainly true rather than to cover us.

What we hold

Because you gave it to us

  • Your name, district, village, phone number and anything you wrote about yourself.
  • Your email address, used only to sign you in. There is no password — we send a one-time code, so there is no password of yours for us to lose.
  • Documents you upload for verification, and photographs you attach.
  • What you list, ask for, pledge, agree and record.

Because the platform made it

  • Reviews other people wrote about you, and the jobs they came from.
  • An append-only record of actions, each entry carrying the hash of the one before it.
  • Fault reports when the app breaks — with emails, phone numbers, record ids and tokens stripped out before they are sent, and a device description no more specific than "Android, Chrome".

Who sees what

Your documents are private. Nobody but you and the verification desk can see them — not other users, not people you deal with. What others see is the verdict: "identity verified". Never the document.
  • Public: your name, district, what you have listed, your rating and how many jobs you have completed, and which documents are verified — as a yes or no, never as a file.
  • The two of you: what you agreed, what was paid, photographs taken at a handover, and anything said in a dispute.
  • MEND GROUP only: your documents, reports you make about others, and fault reports.
  • Nobody: a password, because there isn't one. Card details, because we never take a payment.

Files are kept in private storage. There is no public address for any of them. When one is looked at, a link is made that works for a few minutes and then stops.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and file storage.
  • GitHub serves the pages.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, which means Google sees that a browser asked for a font.

Nobody else. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Not as a promise — there is no code in these platforms that could do it.

Taking a copy

At any time you can download everything held about you as one file: your profile, your documents' details, every job, review, payment, dispute and record entry. The file lists your documents but does not contain them; ask for each one from the app.

Removing your account

You can remove your account yourself. Before anything happens you are shown exactly what will go and what will not, in numbers. You then type your own name to confirm.

Erased

  • Your name, phone, district, village and anything you wrote about yourself.
  • Every document and the files themselves, deleted from storage.
  • Your listings stop being offered.

Kept, without your name attached

  • That jobs happened, payments were confirmed and disputes were settled.
  • Reviews other people wrote — their words about an experience they had.
  • Photographs taken when something was handed over or brought back.
  • The append-only record that those events occurred.
Why those are kept. They are the other person's record as much as yours. If closing an account erased a settled dispute or a handover photograph, then anyone could erase one by closing an account — and every record on these platforms would be worth nothing to the person relying on it. So the link to you is removed and the fact remains.

If that is not acceptable to you, the honest advice is not to use these platforms. We would rather tell you that now than discover it together later.

How long

Your information is kept while your account exists. After removal, what survives is the anonymised record above, kept indefinitely because it is part of somebody else's history. Fault reports are kept for two weeks.

Your other rights

You can correct anything about yourself from the app. You can object to how we handle your information, and you can complain to us or to a data protection authority. Write to info@mendgroup.co.za and a person will answer.

Children

These platforms are for adults. If we learn that an account belongs to a child we will remove it.
