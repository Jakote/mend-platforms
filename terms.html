Mafisa, Pitso and Matsema are three platforms built and owned by MEND GROUP (PTY) LTD, a company registered in Lesotho. They share one account, one verification process and one reputation. Using any of them means accepting these terms.

1. What these platforms are

  • Pitso helps you find someone who does skilled work, and helps them be found.
  • Mafisa is a register of things people are willing to lend, and of the loans made.
  • Matsema lets people pool what they have toward a supply nobody could meet alone.

In every case the agreement is between you and the other person. MEND GROUP is not a party to it, does not employ anyone listed, and does not supply anything listed.

2. Money

These platforms never hold, move, or take a share of your money. Not as a policy we might change — the database has no balance, wallet, escrow or float column anywhere in it, and a test fails the build if one is ever added.

When you record a payment, you are recording that money moved somewhere else — by M-Pesa, EcoCash, bank transfer or cash, between the two of you. Both sides confirm it separately. An electronic payment must carry its transaction code, because that code is what makes the record worth anything.

MEND GROUP charges nothing to use these platforms at present. If that changes you will be told before it does, and not by a change to this page alone.

3. Verification, and what it does not mean

A verified document means a person at MEND GROUP looked at that document and accepted it, on a date, and their decision is recorded against their name. That is all it means.

  • It is not a guarantee that the work will be good.
  • It is not insurance, a licence, or a promise that the person is solvent, safe or honest.
  • For categories where nothing registers ownership at all — a power drill, a generator — the platform verifies who you are and says so in those words. It does not claim the thing is yours, because it cannot know.
  • A document that has passed its expiry is treated as expired, whatever it once said.

Satisfy yourself about the person you are dealing with. These platforms make that easier; they do not do it for you.

4. Disputes

MEND GROUP does not decide who is right. We are not a court and will not act as one.

What the platform does is hold both accounts, dated, and neither can be revised once given — the database refuses to change or delete a statement. A dispute closes only when both people accept the same wording; if the wording changes, any acceptance already given falls away.

Other people see only that a dispute exists and whether it was settled. They never see what was said. An accusation is not a finding.

5. What you may not do

  • List something that is not yours, or that does not exist.
  • Claim a skill, qualification or registration you do not hold.
  • Upload a document belonging to somebody else.
  • Use these platforms to arrange anything unlawful.
  • Harass, threaten or abuse another person.
  • Create accounts in bulk, or post listings faster than a person could. There are limits, and they are enforced by the database rather than by our patience.

6. Reporting and removal

Anyone can report a listing or a person. A report is private to the person who made it and to MEND GROUP; it is never shown to the person reported. What others may see is a count, and only once more than one person has reported the same thing — one report is one opinion.

MEND GROUP may take a listing down. Every removal names the staff member who did it and gives a reason, and that reason is shown publicly — including to you, if it is your listing. That is a deliberate constraint on us, not on you.

7. Your account and your information

You can take a copy of everything these platforms hold about you, at any time, in one file. You can remove your account. What that does, and what it cannot do, is set out in the privacy notice — read it before you rely on it, because some things survive on purpose and you are entitled to know which.

8. Availability

These platforms are provided as they are. They run on free-tier infrastructure, they will sometimes be unavailable, and data may be lost. Keep your own record of anything that matters to you. The platform is designed to make that easy — everything can be shared as plain text or printed.
